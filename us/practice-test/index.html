A click is recognition, not recall. If you scored 18 here but cannot say the same answers out loud without looking, the interview will feel different — read each question aloud and answer before revealing.

Why random, and why 20

Twenty is the maximum the officer will ask, and the draw is random from the whole bank, so a single run can leave out an entire theme. That is the point: three different tests in a row give a far better reading than one long session on the same file. Our PDF has five scored tests on paper, which is closer to the interview than a click.
